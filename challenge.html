Required rules

  • Your video must be posted after this campaign started — videos posted before the campaign began don't count.
  • You must use all of the required hashtags for this challenge.
  • Your video and account must stay public for the full duration of the campaign — private, deleted, or restricted videos cannot be scored.
  • Only one entry per person. Submitting through multiple accounts or tiers will disqualify all related entries.
  • Engagement must be organic. Use of bots, engagement pods, or purchased views/likes/saves/comments/shares will result in disqualification.
  • Content must comply with TikTok's Community Guidelines. Entries that violate them may be removed at any time.
  • All scoring and eligibility decisions made by the EarnOva team are final.

🏆 How the Final Score Is Calculated

Each participant's final score is based on 5 performance metrics, with each metric having a different weight:

Metric Weight
👀 Views10%
❤️ Likes35%
🔖 Saves35%
💬 Comments10%
🔄 Shares10%
Total100%

How it works

For each metric, we compare your number with the combined total of everyone competing in your tier.

The formula is:

Your metric score = (Your metric ÷ Total metric from all participants) × Weight

Then we add the five metric scores together.

Example

Suppose two participants have:

Daniel

  • 100,000 views
  • 44,390 likes
  • 3,988 saves
  • 3,900 comments
  • 127 shares

Emeka

  • 99,888 views
  • 55,100 likes
  • 4,130 saves
  • 4,212 comments
  • 115 shares

For Daniel's views:

100,000 ÷ (100,000 + 99,888) × 10 = 5.01 points

For Daniel's likes:

44,390 ÷ (44,390 + 55,100) × 35 = 15.62 points

The same calculation is done for saves, comments, and shares.

After adding all five:

Daniel = 47.9%
Emeka = 52.1%

📌 Important

The score is calculated within each tier, not by comparing participants across different tiers.

This means the winner is the participant with the highest final percentage in their tier.
