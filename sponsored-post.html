Sponsored post rules

These apply to every campaign you join. Breaking them can get a submission rejected — or a participation disqualified, even after it's been approved or paid out.

  • Post the content yourself, from the social handle you submitted when you joined the campaign.
  • Keep the post live and public for the full review period — don't delete, archive, or make it private before an admin has reviewed it.
  • Meet any minimum engagement requirement listed in the campaign's "Requirements" section.
  • Don't promote the video or run ads — no paid boosts, promotion services, engagement pods, bots, or purchased followers/likes/views to inflate its likes or views. Engagement must be organic.
  • Follow the campaign's content guidelines exactly — don't change required captions, tags, or disclosures.
  • Submit only your own original video link — never someone else's content or a placeholder link.
  • Submit once per campaign. Only resubmit if your first submission was reviewed and rejected.
