Good to know

Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know about earning on EarnOva.

How fast are payouts?

Payouts are instant once you request a withdrawal to your linked bank account.

How much commission can I earn?

You can earn up to 70% commission on affiliate products and referral rewards.

Is there a fee to join?

Creating an account and verifying your email is free. After verification, there's a one-time ₦1,500 activation fee to unlock task access — you're never charged again after that.

Who can join EarnOva?

Anyone in Nigeria with a valid bank account can sign up and start earning.

How do I withdraw my earnings?

Link a bank account and set a withdrawal PIN from your dashboard. Once you have an available balance, request a payout and confirm it with your PIN — funds are sent instantly.

What if I forget my withdrawal PIN?

Head to Settings to reset your PIN. You'll need to verify your identity first to keep your balance secure.

What is the Weekly TikTok Challenge?

Pick your follower tier, post a new TikTok video, and submit your link. Once the campaign ends, your video is scored on views, likes, saves, comments, and shares — the highest score in each tier wins a cash prize.

How are challenge winners chosen?

Each metric is weighted (Likes and Saves count most, at 35% each) and compared against everyone else in your tier. Scores are calculated automatically once results are published — no manual guesswork.

How do surveys work?

Open the Surveys tab and complete any available survey from our partner. Your reward is verified and credited to your balance automatically once you finish.

What is Share & Earn?

Share a link or post to your social media and get paid for it — no minimum followers required, just complete the share.

How do referrals work?

Share your referral code or link with friends. When they sign up and start earning, you get a percentage of what they earn as a referral commission.

Can I have more than one account?

No. Each person may only hold one EarnOva account. Multiple accounts from the same person may be suspended and any pending earnings forfeited.

Is my personal and banking information safe?

Yes. Your data is encrypted and never shared with third parties outside of processing your payouts.
